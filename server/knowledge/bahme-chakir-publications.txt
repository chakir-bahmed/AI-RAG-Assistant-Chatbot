PUBLICATIONS & RESEARCH WORK

Researcher: CHAKIR BAHMED

This section highlights research papers, academic studies, and technical investigations conducted by CHAKIR BAHMED in the fields of Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Software Engineering, Data Engineering, and Emerging Technologies.

The publications demonstrate analytical thinking, research methodology, technical writing, and the ability to explore complex technological challenges through scientific investigation and practical evaluation.

================================================================================
PUBLICATION: RETRIEVAL-AUGMENTED GENERATION FOR INTELLIGENT ASSISTANTS
================================================================================

Title: Design and Development of a RAG-Based Intelligent Assistant

Category: Artificial Intelligence & Large Language Models

Description:
This research explores the architecture, implementation, and evaluation of an intelligent assistant powered by Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG). The study investigates how vector databases, semantic search, and large language models can be combined to produce accurate, context-aware responses. The work also analyzes challenges related to knowledge retrieval, hallucination reduction, and response quality in production AI systems.

PUBLICATION: LARGE-SCALE CHESS ANALYTICS AND PLAYER PERFORMANCE MODELING
================================================================================

Title: Data-Driven Analysis of Competitive Chess Performance

Category: Data Science & Predictive Analytics

Description:
A large-scale analytical study based on more than 500,000 chess games and 600,000 player records. The research investigates player behavior, performance indicators, and ranking evolution. Statistical techniques and data mining methods are applied to identify patterns influencing player success and competitive development.
